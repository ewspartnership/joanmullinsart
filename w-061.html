Sunlight on Red Sandstone Wall

No. 61
Medium

46 × 30 cm

2015
Inspired by

The effect of the sun on old sandstone walls caught my interest during a walk near West Kirby. I loved the way the path seemed to lead towards the sun, the brightness there emphasizing the bright redness of the old walls.
