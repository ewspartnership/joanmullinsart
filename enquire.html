How buying works

  1. Ask. Send a message about the painting you like. Joan will confirm it’s still available, and tell you the postage cost if you’d like it sent.
  2. Agree. Joan emails you the details: the price, postage or collection, and how to pay.
  3. Pay. By bank transfer, or in cash if you collect. Joan sends or hands over the painting once the payment has arrived. [Joan to confirm how she takes payment]
  4. Receive it. Collect it from Nantwich, or Joan posts it within the UK, well packed and by a tracked, insured service.
  5. Change your mind? If it was posted to you, you can return it within 14 days of it arriving for a full refund. Joan’s email confirming the sale explains how.

Every painting is an original by Joan. Works on paper are sold unframed unless the painting’s page says otherwise. [Framing available on request? Joan to say]
