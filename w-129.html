Seascape

No. 129
Medium
Acrylic and ink
Size
36 × 50 cm
Year
2024
Price
£250

I do not use acrylic usually, but decided to as an experiment, as I wanted to convey several levels in this painting: the sea itself, powerful against the unsettling sky; secondly, the outlines of the large shells across the picture; and thirdly, the beach moving strongly from left to right of the painting. I am happy with the result.
